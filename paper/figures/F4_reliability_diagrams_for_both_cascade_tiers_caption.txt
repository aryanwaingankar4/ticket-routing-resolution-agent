Count-weighted binned ECE on the 500-ticket in-distribution production batch. Observed accuracy is 1.0 in EVERY bin, so both tiers are systematically UNDER-confident here and the whole ECE is the gap between confidence and a ceiling. That is a property of template-generated data, not evidence that the models are well calibrated on real traffic -- which is why both escalation gates are calibrated empirically rather than read off a probability.
